Zone Scanner

Real daily bars for stocks and crypto, scored live in this page. Works as a local file or on a public URL.

\n







Zones are found mechanically from daily bars, so check each chart yourself — the rules will
sometimes mark a base where your eye would not. A score says how a setup fits the rules, not how it will
perform. Data comes from public sources through a relay and can be delayed, revised, or wrong; verify prices
with your broker before acting. This is a worksheet, not advice, and not a recommendation of any security.
